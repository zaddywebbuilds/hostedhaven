How It Works

1
Everything saves the same way Open a property or a post, make your changes, and press Save. Nothing goes live until you save.
2
The site rebuilds itself Each save starts a deploy. Your change is live on hostedhavens.co in about 7 minutes. There is nothing else to press.
3
Removing a property Open it under Properties and choose Delete. It disappears from the Stays page and its own page stops existing. Deleting it here does not cancel the listing in Hospitable, so turn it off there too.
!
If a deleted property is used in an article Some articles feature a specific stay. Deleting one of those stops the site updating until the article is changed, on purpose, so guests never hit a dead link. If your change has not appeared after 15 minutes, that is usually why. Send John the property name and he will sort it.
4
Photos for a new property Property photos are prepared in bulk and are not uploaded here yet. Add the property, then send John the photos and he will load them in. Everything else on the page works straight away.
5
The Hospitable listing ID This is what connects the booking calendar to the right listing. Copy it from the listing in Hospitable. If it is wrong, guests will book the wrong stay.
6
Posts publish on their own Every Monday, Wednesday, and Friday at 9am a new article goes live. Edit or delete any of them under Edit Existing Posts.
